## Business Takeaway
The project is designed to show the tradeoff between sales incentives and profitability. Revenue alone
does not tell the full story: region, product mix, customer segment, and discount level should be reviewed
alongside profit and margin before making pricing or sales decisions.

All data is synthetic and created for portfolio analysis.
